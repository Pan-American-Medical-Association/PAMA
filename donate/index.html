FAQs

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. The Pan-American Medical Association is a 501(c)(3) public charity recognized by the IRS, effective December 10, 2024 (EIN 33-2193485). Contributions are tax-deductible as allowed by law. Please check with your tax advisor about your own situation.

Will I get a receipt?

PayPal emails you a receipt as soon as your gift goes through. For checks and supply donations, we send a written acknowledgment for your records.

How are donations used?

Of PAMA’s cash spending, 93.7% has gone directly to medical supplies. Donated supplies are tracked by clinical area, and every shipment is scored with our Solidarity Supply Index. See our financial transparency page for the full breakdown.

What does PAMA do?

PAMA delivers ethical medical aid, trains equity-driven clinicians, and builds infrastructure in underserved communities across the Americas and Caribbean.

Where does PAMA work?

We work with clinicians and partners in Cuba, Guatemala and underserved U.S. communities, including Philadelphia, delivering care where it’s needed most.

Can I volunteer or collaborate?

Yes! We welcome clinicians, logisticians, storytellers, and coalition builders. Reach out to explore how your skills can support our mission.
