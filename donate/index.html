FAQs

What does PAMA do?

PAMA delivers ethical medical aid, trains equity-driven clinicians, and builds infrastructure in underserved communities across the Americas and Caribbean.

How are donations used?

Every donation supports lifesaving shipments, clinical training, and digital health tools. We track impact using our Solidarity Supply Index (SSI) for full transparency.

Can I volunteer or collaborate?

Yes! We welcome clinicians, logisticians, storytellers, and coalition builders. Reach out to explore how your skills can support our mission.

Where does PAMA work?

We operate across Latin America, the Caribbean, and underserved U.S. regions—partnering with local leaders to deliver care where it’s needed most.
