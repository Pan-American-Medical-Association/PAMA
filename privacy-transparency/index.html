Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Pan-American Medical Association. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This privacy policy explains how our application handles user data, including the collection, use, and protection of your personal information.

Information We Collect

Our application collects the following types of information:

  • User Information: When you use our application, we collect email addresses and names to manage membership data for medical professionals.
  • Usage Data: We may collect information about how the application is accessed and used, including interactions with our services.

How We Use Information

The data we collect is used to:

  1. Manage Memberships: We use email addresses and names to update the membership data within the medical professional community.
  2. Improve Our Services: Usage data helps us understand how our application is used and how we can improve our services.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share or disclose your personal information to third parties, except as required by law or to provide the services you requested. All data processing is done in compliance with relevant privacy laws and regulations.

Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our security practices include:

  1. Encryption: Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest to ensure its integrity and confidentiality.
  2. Access Controls: Access to personal information is limited to authorized personnel who need it to perform their job functions.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  1. Access Your Information: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  2. Correct Your Information: Request correction of any inaccuracies in your personal information.
  3. Delete Your Information: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal obligations.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new privacy policy on our website. You are advised to review this privacy policy periodically for any changes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please get in touch with us at Pan-American Medical Association Support: info@pan-american.org
